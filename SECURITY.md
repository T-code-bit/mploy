The **mploy** team takes system security, data privacy, and vulnerability disclosure seriously. We appreciate the efforts of security researchers, customers, and developers in keeping our platform and user data secure.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do NOT report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions, or social media.**

If you discover a potential security flaw, credential leak, or vulnerability within `go-mploy.com` or any associated codebase, report it directly to our security team using any of the following channels:

## Submission Guidelines

To help us evaluate and patch vulnerabilities efficiently, include as much of the following detail as possible in your report:

* **Type of Vulnerability:** (e.g., SQL Injection, XSS, Authentication Bypass, CSRF, Exposure of Sensitive Data).
* **Target Location:** URL, endpoint, or specific code file/component affected.
* **Steps to Reproduce:** Concise step-by-step instructions or a minimal Proof of Concept (PoC) script.
* **Potential Impact:** Explanation of how an attacker could exploit the vulnerability.
* **Suggested Fix:** (Optional) Code modifications or configuration updates that mitigate the issue.

---

## Response & Patch Timelines

We commit to handling security submissions promptly:

1. **Acknowledgment:** Within 24–48 hours of report receipt.
2. **Initial Assessment & Triage:** Within 3 to 5 business days.
3. **Resolution & Patching:** Timeline varies based on severity, but critical issues are prioritized for immediate deployment.
4. **Public Disclosure:** Coordinated with the reporter after a patch has been fully deployed to production.

---

## Safe Harbor & Responsible Disclosure

When conducting research on `go-mploy.com`:

* **Do not** attempt to access, modify, or delete data belonging to other users.
* **Do not** execute Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks or spam service infrastructure.
* **Do not** use automated scanner tools without prior written permission, as they may disrupt production operations.
* **Do** act in good faith to avoid privacy violations and destruction of data.

If you abide by these guidelines during your research, we will not pursue legal action against you regarding your submission.
